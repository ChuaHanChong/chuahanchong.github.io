I am a PhD student at [MMLab@NTU](https://www.mmlab-ntu.com/index.html), College of Computing and Data Science, Nanyang Technological University, advised by [Prof. Chen Change Loy](https://www.mmlab-ntu.com/person/ccloy/) and supported by the NTU Research Scholarship.

My research is on **spatial intelligence**, **embodied AI**, and **real2sim2real**: how an agent builds a spatial representation of the physical world that is good enough to act on, and how to close the gap between what works in simulation and what survives contact with real sensors and real hardware.

I came to this from nine years of building AI systems in industry rather than from a lab. Most recently I led AI engineering at [Porolis Technologies](https://www.porolis.com/), where I architected and ran the production platform behind a suite of vision models, and adapted vision-language foundation models to specialised visual domains under tight annotation budgets. Before that I worked on document understanding at Ideagen, clinical risk modelling at Holmusk, and customer analytics at Celcom Axiata. That history is the reason I care about the sim-to-real gap: it is the failure mode I kept meeting in deployment, and it is not a problem you can benchmark your way out of.

I hold an MSc in Artificial Intelligence from NTU, where my thesis studied self-supervised visual representations for fine-grained maritime vessel recognition, and a BEng in Mechanical Engineering from Universiti Putra Malaysia.
